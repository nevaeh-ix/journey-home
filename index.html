ENTRY 1. DAY ZERO.

The news said it was just a small virus. They lied. The spores are spreading faster than the wind, like a culture taking over a dish, and whatever they touch turns to rust and rot. Panic is hitting the streets right now. I have a full tank and 1000 miles between me and my family. I have to make it home before the world completely stops turning.

Sirens are blaring in the distance.
